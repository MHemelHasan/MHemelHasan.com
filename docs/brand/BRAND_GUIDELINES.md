# M Hemel Hasan — Brand Guidelines v2.0

## Brand direction
Approved direction: **Concept C — Wordmark + Monogram**.

Concept C is the single source of truth. Concepts A and B are historical alternatives.

## Logo system
Use the canonical stacked SVG wherever the Concept C signature lockup fits.
Use the horizontal lockup when vertical space is limited.
Use the standalone MH monogram for favicon, avatar, app icon, compact navigation, and small-format identity use.

### Geometry rules
- Do not replace the monogram with typed M and H.
- Do not straighten the diagonal interlock.
- Do not turn the H into a generic full-height left stem.
- Do not round the sharp corners.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, outline, shadow, bevel, or add gradients.

## Clear space
Standalone monogram: minimum clear space = 0.25× monogram height.
Lockups: keep at least one wordmark-cap-height of clear space.

## Minimum size
- Monogram: 16px digital minimum.
- Stacked lockup: 160px digital width recommended minimum.
- Horizontal lockup: 220px digital width recommended minimum.

## Color
- Primary cobalt: `#315CF5`
- Hover: `#264AC9`
- Soft cobalt: `#EEF2FF`
- Ink: `#111318`
- Muted text: `#646A73`
- Warm background: `#FAF9F6`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Border: `#E6E2DC`

Light-background logo: cobalt M + ink H/wordmark.
Dark-background logo: cobalt M + white H/wordmark.
Use monochrome assets when color is unavailable.

## Typography
Primary: **Geist Sans**
Secondary / technical: **Geist Mono**

Geist Mono is for real technical metadata, code, IDs, and system values only.

## Visual direction
Precise, modern, engineering-led, and human.
Use warm neutrals as the foundation and cobalt as a controlled identity/accent color.
Avoid generic SaaS gradients, glow-heavy UI, and “blue everywhere”.
