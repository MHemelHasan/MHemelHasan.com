## 1. Primary Brand Identity

- **Name:** M Hemel Hasan
- **Core Title / Descriptor:** Product Engineer. Builder. Founder.
- **Core Message / Positioning:**  
  > *"I turn product ideas into production software — from research and architecture to backend systems, integrations and launch."*

### The Identity in Plain English
M Hemel Hasan is an end-to-end product engineer and venture builder who takes product ideas from initial ambiguity into production. He investigates platform constraints, models domain data, builds reliable backend systems and APIs, integrates third-party ecosystems, and crafts responsive, polished interfaces. He moves comfortably between product strategy, technical architecture, and hands-on execution.

## 2. Target Audience

The website is designed for product-oriented people who value thoughtful execution:

| Audience Segment | Who They Are | What They Care About | How We Connect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Startup Founders & Co-Founders** | Early-stage and growing venture founders seeking a technical partner or senior product builder. | Product viability, execution speed, architectural integrity, and founder mindset. | Proving founder empathy through **Social AI** and demonstrating an end-to-end build pipeline. |
| **Product & Engineering Leaders** | Leaders at product companies and platform businesses (Shopify, Webflow, WordPress, SaaS). | Ecosystem depth, clean APIs, data integrity, maintainability, and reliable shipping. | Showcasing deep product work at Themefic (Shopify apps, Webflow apps, WordPress plugins). |
| **Collaborators & Fellow Builders** | Builders, engineers, and operators exploring shared software projects. | Craft, technical honesty, and hands-on track record. | Open, human, and approachable dialogue with zero pretension. |

## 3. Brand Personality & Tone of Voice

The website should feel impressive because of the work and interaction quality—not because the copy boasts about being impressive.

| Dimension | Our Tone | What We Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Posture** | Calm, credible, experienced, confident, approachable, human. | Pompous, self-important, salesy, or overly casual. |
| **Language** | Direct, clear, product-focused, outcome-oriented. | Buzzwords like "rockstar", "ninja", "elite", "museum-grade", "zero hand-holding". |
| **Technical Depth** | Grounded in real systems, platform constraints, and architecture. | Surface-level tech-soup lists or generic logo walls. |
| **Founder Energy** | Genuine curiosity, building in the open, skin in the game. | Exaggerated claims, fabricated metrics, or hype-driven startup theater. |

## 5. What the Brand IS vs. What the Brand IS NOT

### What the Brand IS:
1. **A Product Engineer:** Someone who cares deeply about user workflows, product mechanics, and platform constraints alongside backend architecture.
2. **A Founder:** Actively building and launching independent software products (**Social AI**), proving ongoing curiosity and ownership.
3. **A Systems Builder:** Experienced with data modeling, backend services, APIs, authentication, webhook pipelines, and integrations.
4. **An Ecosystem Specialist:** Skilled in the specific mechanics of platforms like Shopify (Remix, Polaris, Admin GraphQL), Webflow (Apps, APIs), and WordPress (plugins, REST API, extensibility).
5. **A Collaborative Peer:** Approachable, thoughtful, and ready to engage in meaningful conversations about building products.

### What the Brand IS NOT:
1. **NOT a Freelancer Portfolio:** No hourly rates, no "Hire Me" buttons, no transactional gig language.
2. **NOT a Job-Seeker Resume:** No "Download Resume" buttons, no punch-card job history, no passive job-hunting language.
3. **NOT an Agency Website:** Does not pretend to be a full-service marketing agency with rate cards or service menus.
4. **NOT a Pure Frontend Slicer:** Does not sell simple HTML/CSS conversion. Interface polish is supported by backend and product logic.
5. **NOT a Tech-Stack Wall:** Avoids displaying 40 disconnected framework badges. Technologies appear in the context of real systems.

## 6. Primary Call-to-Action (CTA) Philosophy

- **Philosophy:** Approachable, peer-level connection. Visitors are invited to start a constructive conversation around software products, technical collaborations, or product builds.
- **Primary CTA Copy:** `Start a Conversation`
- **Hero / Interactive Trigger:** `Ask me about what I build…` with prompt chips.
- **Collaboration Framing:**
  > *"I’m open to thoughtful product work, technical collaborations, and conversations around software products."*
- **Direct Channels Provided:** Verified email (`hello@mhemelhasan.com`), verified LinkedIn, and verified GitHub.
